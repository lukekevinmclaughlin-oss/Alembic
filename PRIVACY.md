# Alembic Privacy Policy

Alembic does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or the contents of imported datasets. Deterministic data processing runs on the device. If a user explicitly configures a third-party model provider, only the content they choose to send is transmitted directly to that provider under its terms. Subscription purchases are processed by Apple.
